THE PROBLEM
In rural Nigeria, distance is the disease.
Most Nigerians who die of treatable illness do not die because the medicine does not exist. They die because the road to it is too long, too expensive, or too late at night to travel.

WHY THIS ONE WORKS HERE
Built for what you have, not what you are missing.
Most telemedicine assumes a smartphone, a data plan, a card and a shared language. In rural Nigeria that is four assumptions too many.
Works on a phone with no internet
Dial *737# and book a doctor through a menu, the same way you buy airtime. No app, no data, no smartphone.
Works when the network is down
Your health worker's phone keeps working offline and sends everything the moment there is signal again.
Pay the way you already pay
MTN MoMo, OPay, bank transfer — or cash handed to your health worker, receipted on the spot.
Speaks the way you speak
Igbo, Hausa, Yorùbá, Pidgin and English. Every screen will read itself out loud if you ask it to.
THE PERSON IN THE MIDDLE
A trained neighbour,
not a stranger.
SHATS does not drop an app into a village and hope. Every community has a Community Health Worker — someone from there, trained and equipped — who registers people, takes blood pressure and temperature, sits in on the consultation, and carries the cases a phone alone cannot carry.
Registers people who have no ID and no fixed address
Works offline all day, then syncs everything at night
Escalates to a doctor in seconds, not days
WHO YOU WILL MEET
Every doctor is checked against the register before they see anyone.
Licence verified with the Medical and Dental Council of Nigeria, identity confirmed, and every consultation recorded. If a doctor is struck off, their account closes the same day.

SAFETY AND PRIVACY
Your record belongs to you, not to a clinic.
Rural patients are used to their history living in a paper file in a building they cannot get back to. On SHATS it travels with them.
It follows you
Your history goes with you to any doctor you choose, on SHATS or off it.
Nobody looks without asking
A doctor sees your record only for the consultation you booked. Every opening is logged.
NDPR compliant
Built to the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation. Consent is recorded before anything is shared.
It knows about NHIS
If you are enrolled, your cover is applied before you are ever asked to pay.

ABOUT THIS PROJECT
SHATS is a university design project (CSC 411, Object-Oriented Analysis & Design). It is not a real service, it is not registered with any health authority, and it cannot be used to obtain medical care or advice. Doctor and patient names are illustrative. Some photographs are AI-generated and do not depict real people. Statistics are cited from WHO and World Bank sources and were checked in August 2026.
